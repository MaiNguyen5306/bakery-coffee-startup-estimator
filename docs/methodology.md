# Methodology · model 0.5.0
Funding totals combine evidence-derived role wages/food fees with unsupported demo assumptions. Low/high are consistent illustrative configurations. They are not measured percentiles or a statistical interval. No midpoint is treated as an expected value.

Rent = sqft × (annual base + annual additional rent) / 12. The deposit assumes the same rent basis and is refundable, separate from expense. Preopening rent covers only months before operations; month 1 operating rent is paid in reserve. No first/last operating month prepayment or tenant allowance is assumed.

Buildout = sqft × condition rate. Demo scope includes building trades; equipment hookups and professional design are separately budgeted. Package inclusion must be reconciled before replacing with a quote. No user quote support yet. An existing restaurant still needs compliance review.

Equipment is selected by production package, quantity and tier. Premium changes only equipment prices. All equipment is purchased new in this model; used prices are unsupported. Tax applies only to equipment and its freight/hookups under an explicit unverified demo basis. Freight/tax never apply to payroll, deposits or official fees. Other opening licenses remain a demo allowance, explicitly excluding food plan review. Food plan-review and annual operating-permit values use the official 2026 county table with user-assumed applicability, not a regulatory determination.

Paid role hours × 52/12 × hourly wage gives base payroll. Employer expense uses a labeled percentage approximation; no claim of an official aggregate tax rate. Training uses whole-team hours once before opening. Service requirement = days × (2 × open hours + 2 setup/close hours). Owner coverage is capped at the lesser of selected hours or days × (open hours + 1), and allocated to one service position only. The owner cannot cover simultaneous positions. Presets can leave service coverage gaps, shown separately and not secretly filled. Production adds 0/20/60 weekly paid hours for purchased/limited/on-site. Minimum headcount = ceil(role hours/40); overtime and actual staffing schedules are unresolved.

Monthly planned-activity budget includes operating rent, payroll, baseline utilities, services, maintenance, replenishment, optional owner draw and monthly equivalents of insurance/annual permits. Production adds explicit 20%/60% demo utilities and replenishment allowances; other categories are unchanged. These usage assumptions are not verified bills or supplier quotes. Opening stock is separate from subsequent months' replenishment. No revenue collections are offset.

Reserve cash schedules modeled payments month by month. Insurance is paid before opening for months 1–12, then renewed in month 13, 25, etc. Food operating permits are paid before opening at the official quarter fraction: Apr–Jun100%,Jul–Sep75%,Oct–Dec50%,Jan–Mar25%,covering through March31. Renewal cash is allocated in March before April coverage based on official historical March31 deadline guidance; actual current invoice date is still to be confirmed. March31 openings include the same-day next-period renewal before opening and never again in that month’s reserve. Future rates are held at2026 as an explicit assumption. Month1 is the opening calendar month with a full monthly operating allowance; partial-month rent/payroll are not prorated. Monthly equivalents are excluded from cash while prepaid. Fixed-only reserve excludes replenishment; it still includes the planned payroll, utilities and services and annual renewals. Zero months yields zero reserve.

Contingency applies once to buildout, equipment (including freight/tax), training, initial inventory, professional services and launch. It excludes refundable deposits, occupancy, reserve and fixed fee allowances. Before-opening cash is reported before contingency; total funding = before-opening cash + contingency + reserve.

Selection and evidence: research totals are blocked globally until a versioned, validated applicable assumption set exists. Header-only tables pass schema checks but do not imply research completeness. Do not auto-promote file contents into defaults. Sources need effective dates, provenance and applicability. Rent comparables must normalize quote periods, retain missing CAM and deduplicate addresses; convenience-sample min/max is not a population interval. BLS metro role medians remain benchmarks, not Seattle city wage rules. The official 2026 Seattle floor of $21.30/hour is enforced on both role wage endpoints, including user overrides; unsupported years are rejected. Source reuse terms must be reviewed before distribution.

The React interface sends scenario inputs and overrides to `/api/estimate`, a Vercel Python Function using the same independent calculation package. No financial calculations are duplicated in the frontend. Saved baseline comparisons remain in browser memory; no account or server persistence is used.

Wage derivation: service uses May2025 metro35-3023 H_MEDIAN18.82 and H_PCT7521.75; baker uses51-3011 median23.16 and75th25.80. Low=max(role median,2026 Seattle21.30floor);high=max(role75th,floor). No averaging across roles or inflation extrapolation. This is an editorial scenario band, not a confidence interval or Seattle-only survey. Reproduce with scripts/build_assumptions.py then scripts/derive_wage_assumptions.py. Employer burden,coverage requirements and overtime remain assumed. Reserve projections hold selected-year wages/costs constant; they do not establish future legal wage rules.

Food plan review: existing/partial conditions assume remodel base756USD(3hours);shell assumes new-construction base1008USD(4hours). Additional user-entered billable hours ×252USD/hour are added. Scope/classification must be confirmed with the agency. Plan review is a separate before-opening line paid at plan submission; the operating permit is paid after application processing before the pre-operational inspection. Other fees/building permits are not implied to be covered by the food fee. Food permit risk1 is incompatible with on-site cooking/baking; default risk2 is a planning choice, not an assigned classification.

Revenue methodology and evidence contracts are in revenue_methodology.md and revenue_data_dictionary.md. The cost-to-revenue adapter explicitly excludes startup,owner draws and replenishment,uses annual monthly allocations,and propagates evidence status into operating results.

Staffing update (0.5.0): employee hours are selected explicitly. Number of staff excludes the owner. All selected employees use either the assumed 20-hour part-time schedule or the assumed 40-hour full-time schedule. Contracted paid hours = staff count × selected weekly hours. Owner hours never reduce these paid commitments. The default is two full-time employees. A mixed part/full-time roster is not modeled. Advanced additional service hours are added explicitly beyond the selected staff-count budget and are separately identified in the export and formula.

Owner operated defaults to 40 owner service hours; Staff led defaults to 0. These presets change owner/supervisor planning defaults, not the selected staff count or contracted hours. Owner coverage is capped at one position across open hours plus one setup/closing hour per day. Applied coverage is separate from employee payroll and owner cash draw.

Role allocation is within the paid budget: allocate up to the demo production requirement (0/20/60 weekly hours for purchased/limited/on-site production), then working-supervisor hours, then the remainder to regular service. A production shortfall is flagged and blocks complete linked break-even. Automatic staff-led supervisor request = max(0,days × (open hours+1) - applied owner service hours); owner-operated automatic request = 0. Applied supervisor hours cannot exceed the paid hours left after production. Requested and applied supervisor hours are displayed separately. Supervisors are not counted twice. Missing service or staff-led leadership coverage also blocks complete linked break-even. Extra service hours increase regular service only and do not conceal a missing baker or supervisor.

Working supervisor wages remain May 2025 Seattle metro SOC 35-1012 median 29.62 and 75th percentile 36.57 USD/hour, clamped to the registered 2026 Seattle floor. This is a working shift lead, not a dedicated general manager or a researched hiring quote. Role payroll = weekly paid role hours × 52/12 × role wage; employer allowance applies once. Payroll_by_role in exports reconciles to aggregate payroll. Coverage requirements, headcount schedules and employer burden remain planning/demo assumptions.

Example: three part-time employees supply 60 total paid hours/week; three full-time employees supply 120. Owner hours do not change either total, but affect uncovered service hours and automatic supervisor allocation. Four full-time bakery employees supply 160 hours total: with a 60-hour production requirement and a 60-hour automatic supervisor requirement, 40 hours remain for regular service. These hours are not one person's schedule. Minimum qualified supervisor counts use the selected 20/40-hour schedule; actual shifts, qualifications, overtime and simultaneous coverage require a roster review.

Model 0.5.1 standard scope: both coffee shops and retail bakeries include one commercial espresso machine and two grinders. There is no opt-out or separately invented flat service fee. Equipment purchase prices, tier selection and the existing tax/freight/hookup allowances apply normally. Prices and associated usage/capacity remain demo planning assumptions. This is a selected model configuration, not an observed claim about all Seattle shops.
