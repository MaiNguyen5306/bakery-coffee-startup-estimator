# Revenue methodology · model 0.3.0
Revenue is scenario arithmetic, not observed demand or a sales forecast. Net sales exclude sales tax and tips and are net of discounts/refunds represented in AOV. Orders are transactions, not unique customers. Operating surplus is not owner take-home pay.

The basic calculator requires positive AOV, exact integer open days (1–31) and positive daily opening hours (up to 24). Daily order input is constant across open days. The optional weekly helper computes round(weekly days × 52/12), capped at 31, and leaves the result editable; it is not a calendar count. Initial Revenue hours use the shared Cost opening schedule. Shared shop settings remain in the root React component. Revenue-specific monthly days can differ from the weekly approximation but an expanded schedule beyond linked staffing blocks break-even pending review.

Monthly orders N = daily orders × days. Sales = N × AOV. Required monthly orders = target sales / AOV. Required orders/day = target / (AOV × days); orders/hour divides again by hours. Zero target is valid. Financial calculations are never rounded; ceil(monthly orders) is the minimum whole-month count, while ceil(daily orders) is an optional constant daily goal. Repeated rounded hourly goals can overshoot.

Optional baskets require shares summing to 1 and positive item quantities. AOV = Σ share × basket sales; product cost/order = Σ share × basket product cost. The interface offers an editable drink-only and drink-plus-pastry mix; all mix, quantities, costs and default prices are assumptions, not inferred observed AOV. The API also supports explicit basket sales/costs and per-item quantities. Reference menu prices are displayed separately and are never silently pooled or adopted.

Recipe costs use supplier pack quantities and compatible mass/volume/count units. Effective ingredient input cost = base cost/(1-input loss). Divide batch input cost by sellable yield, then add packaging per applicable sellable yield. Yield must not already incorporate the same loss. Purchased pastries use purchase costs and waste, not invented recipes. No supplier/recipe observations were supplied; schemas stay empty and cannot establish zero product cost. The basic calculator works without them; break-even requires an explicit user acknowledgement that assumed product cost includes waste and packaging once.

Payment fee/order = Σ transaction share × [channel AOV × (1 + fee-base uplift) × percentage + fixed transaction fee]. Channel shares sum to 1. Default channel AOV equals scenario AOV; explicit unequal channel baskets must reconcile to overall AOV. Fee uplift is editable to account for collected sales tax/tips on which processing may be charged. Card/cash share is assumed. Square Free in-person and online rates have distinct observed source records, US market, access date and channel. Rates were verified on the official web page, not extracted from the PDF. No effective dates were supplied, so they are blank. The UI offers Free or user-entered rates; Plus/Premium are available as evidence but not automatic selections because paid subscriptions would need reconciliation with the linked services allowance. Monthly subscription cost must appear once among fixed expenses, never in per-order fees. Foreign card, special tender, delivery marketplace commissions and volume pricing are outside the selected rate's scope.

Cost integration preserves stable line IDs and classifies: occupancy/utilities/services/maintenance/annual equivalents as fixed allocations, base payroll as a stepped cost held fixed only within the assumed schedule/capacity, product costs as per-order, percentage costs as sales-dependent, and processing fixed fees as per-order. Linked replenishment is excluded because Revenue replaces it with explicit product costs. Owner draws, startup lines, deposits and reserve funding are excluded. Annual permit and insurance expenses are monthly operating allocations here; Cost separately schedules actual prepayments/renewals. No hypothetical sales reduce startup reserve. No extra POS subscription is added automatically. Incomplete service coverage, expanded staffing schedules or missing variable-cost confirmation block break-even while allowing basic sales arithmetic.

Contribution/order = AOV × (1-r) - product cost/order - payment fee/order. F is the linked recurring fixed-cost endpoint or a labeled user-entered complete fixed cost. Break-even orders = F / contribution, sales = orders × AOV. Operating surplus = net sales - product/processing/percentage costs - F. Orders for desired operating surplus P = (F+P) / contribution. Nonpositive contribution produces no finite break-even. Debt service, income tax, depreciation and owner draws are excluded. Low/high refer to fixed-cost endpoints; they are not profit confidence bounds.

Peak rate = daily orders × peak share / peak hours. Nonpeak rate = daily orders × (1-share)/(hours-peak hours), when nonpeak hours exist. An all-day peak interval must include all orders. Compare average, peak and nonpeak to user-entered effective shop capacity; no employee parallelism is invented. Break-even and target-surplus requirements exceeding that scope are flagged, not treated as staffing-valid operating plans.

Cost and Revenue inputs form a snapshot signature. Any changed Cost or Revenue input hides old Revenue figures and exports while an automatic recalculation runs after a 250ms debounce. Server exports include shared shop, selected assumptions, linked Cost result/fingerprint, channels, baskets, formulas and model/data versions. Replay verifies versions, linked Cost reproducibility and resulting figures. Baseline comparisons list changed Revenue decisions and exact channel/basket/shop snapshots. Sales sensitivity changes only AOV and daily orders with days fixed.

Unsupported cost inputs retain demo status. Revenue results inherit demo status whenever linked recurring inputs include demo/assumed lines and remain labeled illustrative. User-entered fixed costs are assumed, never promoted to researched evidence by calculation.

Staffing0.4.0:linked payroll includes separate working-supervisor hours at a supervisor wage. Supervisors replace general-service coverage,not add the same coverage twice. Missing staff-led owner/supervisor coverage now blocks complete linked break-even,as does uncovered general service. Revenue snapshots inherit these staffing changes and recalculate automatically.

Model 0.5.0 staffing link: the paid team is now explicit count × assumed 20/40-hour schedules, plus any separately entered extra service hours. Owner service coverage is separate and does not reduce contracted employee hours. Production and supervisor hours allocate within that pool; production shortfalls also block complete linked break-even. Headcount/schedule changes invalidate prior linked Revenue snapshots and recalculate automatically.

Automatic updates replace the original manual-refresh requirement at the owner’s request. Each calculation captures one immutable input snapshot and obtains freshly calculated linked costs from the API. Previous timers/requests are cancelled, and late responses are ignored. Invalid inputs show an error with no stale results or downloads until corrected.
